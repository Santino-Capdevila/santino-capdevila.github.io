Wordflag

Style guide & word list highlighter for Google Docs

Highlight the words your client's style guide bans, your brand terms, and hard-to-read writing, right inside Google Docs. Your text never leaves Google.

Coming soon to the Google Workspace Marketplace. Questions? santinocloud00@gmail.com

What it does

Your own word lists

Paste the words or phrases from a client's style guide, banned words or brand terms. Wordflag highlights every match in the color you pick (whole words, not case-sensitive).

Readability marks

Long and very long sentences, passive voice, -ly adverbs, and weak words or wordy phrases, each in its own color. Turn any category on or off.

Readability grade

A Flesch-Kincaid grade level and a count of every type of mark.

One-click Clear

Clear removes Wordflag's highlights and restores your document's original highlight colors.

Whole document or selection

Analyze everything, or only the text you select.

Private by design

Wordflag runs inside Google's own Apps Script platform. Your document text is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Free plan

Every readability mark, with no word limit, plus one word list of up to 50 words or phrases. A Pro plan with unlimited lists is planned; it is not available yet.

Good to know

  • English only.
  • Wordflag analyzes the main body of the current tab, including tables and lists. Headers, footers and footnotes are not analyzed and are never changed.
  • Highlights are a snapshot: if you edit the text after Analyze, run Analyze again. Text typed inside a highlighted area may keep the highlight color after Clear.
  • Passive voice and adverb detection use rules, not AI, so they can miss some cases or flag a few that are fine.
