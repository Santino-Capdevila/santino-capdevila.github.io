Privacy Policy

Wordflag · Last updated October 12, 2026

In short: Wordflag reads the document you are working on only to highlight it, inside Google's own servers. Your document text is never sent to us or to anyone else. We do not sell, share or use your data for advertising.

Who we are

Wordflag is a Google Docs add-on made by Santino Capdevila, an independent developer based in Argentina ("we"). Contact: santinocloud00@gmail.com.

What Wordflag can access, and why

When you install Wordflag, Google asks you to allow these permissions. Wordflag uses them only for the purpose shown:

PermissionHow Wordflag uses it
The document you are using
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/documents.currentonly		Read the text of the document where you open Wordflag, and change the highlight color of the matches. Wordflag also stores hidden markers (named ranges) inside that document that remember the original highlight colors, so Clear can restore them; Clear deletes the markers. Wordflag cannot see or open any other file in your Google Drive.
Display the sidebar
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/script.container.ui		Show the Wordflag panel inside Google Docs.
Your email address and basic profile
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email and .../userinfo.profile		The Google Workspace Marketplace adds these two permissions to every app it installs. Wordflag does not read, store or use your email address or profile.

Wordflag reads your document only when you click Analyze or Clear in its sidebar. The analysis runs in Google Apps Script, on Google's servers. Wordflag has no servers of its own.

What is stored, and where

  • Your settings. Your word lists, colors and category choices are saved with Google's Apps Script user properties, inside your own Google account, so they are available in every document. We, the developer, cannot read them. The Reset settings link in the sidebar deletes them at any time.
  • Markers inside your document. To restore your original highlight colors, Wordflag stores hidden named ranges in the document you analyze. They are part of that document, stored by Google like the rest of it, and Clear deletes them.
  • Error reports. If the add-on hits an error, Google Apps Script may record a technical error report (the function name, the error message and the time) in our Google Cloud project. We use these reports only to fix bugs. They do not contain your document's text. Google keeps them for a limited time (30 days by default).

What we do not do

  • We do not receive, store or read the text of your documents.
  • We do not sell, rent, transfer or share your data with anyone.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, credit decisions or to train AI models.
  • The sidebar has no analytics, tracking or advertising code.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Wordflag's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Paid plans

Wordflag is free. A paid Pro plan is planned but not available. Before it launches we will update this policy to describe exactly what it uses.

Keeping or deleting your data

  • Click Reset settings in the sidebar to delete your word lists and settings.
  • Click Clear in a document to remove Wordflag's highlights and markers from it.
  • Uninstall Wordflag from Extensions > Add-ons > Manage add-ons, and remove its access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Questions or requests: santinocloud00@gmail.com.

Children

Wordflag is a tool for professional writing and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its date. If a change affects how your data is used, we will tell you in the add-on and ask for your consent first.
