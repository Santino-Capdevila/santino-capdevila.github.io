Frequently asked questions

How do I start?

Open a document in Google Docs, choose Extensions > Wordflag > Open Wordflag, type your words in the word list and click Analyze.

How do I remove the highlights?

Click Clear in the sidebar. Your original highlight colors come back.

Does Wordflag change my text?

No. It only changes highlight (background) colors, and Clear restores them.

Is my document sent anywhere?

No. The analysis runs on Google's servers as part of the add-on. Nothing is sent to us or to third parties.

Why are some words not highlighted?

Matches are whole words: "client" matches "client" and "client's", but not "clients". Add each form you want to catch. Headers, footers and footnotes are not analyzed.

The panel says Google could not connect it to my account. What do I do?

This is a known limitation of Google Docs add-ons when several Google accounts are signed in to the same browser. Open the document in a browser window or profile where only the account that owns the document is signed in, or sign in to that account first so it becomes the default.

How many words can my list have?

The free plan includes one list of up to 50 words or phrases.

How do I delete my data?

Click Reset settings at the bottom of the sidebar to delete your word lists and settings. Click Clear in a document to remove Wordflag's highlights and markers from it. You can then uninstall Wordflag from Extensions > Add-ons > Manage add-ons.

Known limitations

  • English only.
  • Wordflag analyzes the main body of the current tab, including tables and lists. Headers, footers and footnotes are not analyzed and are never changed.
  • Highlights are a snapshot: if you edit the text after Analyze, run Analyze again. Text typed inside a highlighted area may keep the highlight color after Clear.
  • Passive voice and adverb detection use rules, not AI, so they can miss some cases or flag a few that are fine.
